YOUR WINDOW INTO ORBIT

Find something interesting

Search by name or NORAD ID, browse the list, or select a dot on the map. Catalogs choose the data; filters narrow what you see.

02

Follow its path

The solid line is the past half of an orbit; the dashed line is the future half. Drag to explore, scroll or pinch to zoom.

03

Let time move

Pause for a closer look or speed up the simulation. Select Now to return to the present at normal speed.

Positions are calculated from public orbital elements, not live telemetry. Older elements and times far from their epoch are less reliable. “Above horizon” does not guarantee a satellite is visible to the eye.
